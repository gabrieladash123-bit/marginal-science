# Marginal

## Register
product

## Users and purpose
Researchers refining an English scientific abstract, and technical reviewers assessing the creator's ML system design. A visitor should be able to edit a manuscript, inspect measured text-classification evidence, review precise revisions, and trace the model's evaluation and deployment.

## Personality
Considered, precise, humane.

## Design principles
- Put the manuscript before the score.
- Make edits explicit and reversible.
- Separate empirical classification evidence from writing advice.
- Keep dataset provenance and actual model measurements inspectable.
- Preserve citations and quantities; require the author to review scientific meaning.
- Keep revision history and claim changes inspectable in a local research notebook.
- Expose model sensitivity with reproducible probes and explicit scope limits.

## Anti-references
No alarmist plagiarism-checker UI, guaranteed authorship claims, detector-evasion claims, invented benchmark numbers, or generic neon AI landing page.

## Accessibility
WCAG AA contrast, visible keyboard focus, semantic controls, reduced motion, and responsive layouts at 375, 768, and 1440 pixels.
